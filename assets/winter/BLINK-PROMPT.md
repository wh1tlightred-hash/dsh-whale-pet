最终提示词：

Edit this exact transparent desktop pet sprite to produce ONE closed-eye blink frame. Preserve the exact canvas size, character scale, position, silhouette, all hair, hat, clothing, hands, feet, colors and mouth. ONLY change the girl's two human eyes to naturally gently closed eyelids, dark curved lash lines. Keep whale hat eyes open unchanged. Do not move or redraw any other part. This is a frame swapped for 140 milliseconds in an animation, so pixel alignment is critical. Full character, transparent background, same square canvas. No text.
